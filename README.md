## What is the Pool?

The **Piscine** is an intensive 26‑day training period at 42 School.  
It is designed to test your ability to learn independently and work under pressure.

 No traditional teachers  
 Peer‑to‑peer evaluation  
 Self‑learning through research 

 Focus areas:  
   **C programming**  
   **Algorithmic thinking**  
   **Problem‑solving**

## Exams Preparation

42 exams change every year, but the main thing remains the same: **problem solving**.

The exercises here may look random because they are mainly focused on practicing how to think, analyze, and solve problems.

You can also practice on **LeetCode** or **Codewars** to improve your problem-solving skills.

> Focus on learning how to solve problems, not memorizing exercises.
